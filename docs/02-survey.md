## Why only parents

Parents are the easiest user group to reach, and in this system they own the child's data. The specialists' side (H1, H4) will be checked through conversations with 1–2 specialists and with the supervisor.

## Distribution

The link was shared through the author's personal network (friends, relatives and their acquaintances with children). This is a convenience sample, which is noted as a limitation in `02-relevance.md`.

## Data protection

Only the summary of results (charts) is stored in the repository. Individual answers and the contacts from question 12 are kept only in the author's Google account and are not published.
